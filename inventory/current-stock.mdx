The Inventory module in Hitayu ERP gives you a single, unified view of your product stock across every warehouse you operate. From live quantity counts to a full audit trail of every item that has moved in or out, this section is your central hub for keeping stock levels accurate and your fulfilment running smoothly.

## Navigating to Inventory

Go to **Admin → Inventory** (`/admin/inventory/current-stock`). The Inventory section is split into three segments you can switch between using the tabs at the top of the page:

| Segment | URL | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Current Stock** | `/admin/inventory/current-stock` | Live per-product, per-warehouse quantities |
| **Stock Ledger** | `/admin/inventory/stock-ledger` | Complete history of all stock movements |
| **Stock Audit** | `/admin/inventory/stock-audit` | Formal reconciliation records |

<Note>
  Your access to each segment depends on the permissions assigned to your role. Contact your administrator if a tab is missing or greyed out.
</Note>

## Current Stock View

The **Current Stock** tab shows every product variant alongside its available quantity in each warehouse. Each row in the table represents a unique combination of product variant and warehouse location.

### Columns at a glance

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Product** | The parent product name |
| **Variant** | The specific SKU or variant (size, colour, etc.) |
| **Warehouse(s)** | The warehouse(s) holding this stock, shown per location |
| **Total Stock** | Total units physically present across all warehouses |
| **Reserved** | Units held against open orders and not yet shipped |
| **Available** | Total stock minus reservations — what can still be sold |

<Tip>
  Use the search bar and warehouse filter at the top of the table to quickly narrow down results when managing a large catalogue.
</Tip>

### Low-Stock Indicators

When a variant's **Available** quantity falls to a low level, the row is highlighted and a warning badge appears beside the quantity. This allows your purchasing team to react quickly before a stockout occurs. A separate badge marks variants that have reached zero available stock — these require immediate attention.

<Note>
  Stock status badges update automatically as orders are placed and goods are received. No manual configuration is needed for the indicators to appear.
</Note>

### How Stock Reservations Work

When a customer places an order, Hitayu ERP immediately creates a **stock reservation** for each item in that order. Reserved units are subtracted from the available quantity shown in the current-stock view but remain physically in the warehouse until the shipment is dispatched.

- A reservation is **active** from the moment an order is confirmed.
- It is **released** automatically when the order is shipped or cancelled.
- If an order is cancelled, the reserved quantity returns to available stock without any manual intervention.

This prevents overselling across all your connected sales channels in real time.

## Stock Ledger

The **Stock Ledger** is an immutable, time-stamped log of every quantity change that has ever occurred for any product variant in your inventory. Think of it as an accounting journal, but for stock.

### What the ledger records

| Movement Type | Triggered by |
|---|---|
| **Inward (MRN)** | Goods received against a Purchase Order |
| **Outward (Order)** | Items dispatched for a customer order |
| **Reservation** | Stock held when an order is placed |
| **Release** | Reservation freed on cancellation or return |
| **Adjustment (Audit)** | Corrections made when a stock audit is completed |
| **Bootstrap** | Initial stock imported from Shiprocket |

### Filtering the ledger

Use the filters at the top of the Stock Ledger page to narrow down entries:

- **Date range** — view movements within a specific period
- **Warehouse** — isolate a single location
- **Product / Variant** — drill into one SKU
- **Movement type** — show only inward, outward, or adjustment entries
- **Source channel** — filter by the e-commerce channel that triggered the movement

<Tip>
  Export the filtered ledger to CSV using the **Export** button on the top-right for reporting or reconciliation with your accounting software.
</Tip>

## Bootstrapping Initial CRM Stock

If you are setting up Hitayu ERP for the first time and already have stock recorded in Shiprocket, you can import those quantities into the CRM in a single action — no manual data entry required.

<Warning>
  Bootstrap CRM Stock is a **one-time import**. Running it more than once will create duplicate ledger entries and distort your stock history. Only use this action when initialising a brand-new inventory setup.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Bootstrap dialog">
    Navigate to **Admin → Inventory → Current Stock** (`/admin/inventory/current-stock`). Click the **Bootstrap CRM Stock** button in the top-right area of the page. The page will scroll to the launch section automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pre-flight summary">
    Hitayu ERP will display the number of product variants and warehouses it is about to import from Shiprocket. Verify the counts match what you expect before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the import">
    Click **Confirm Bootstrap**. The system will pull current stock quantities from Shiprocket and create corresponding warehouse-stock records and ledger entries in Hitayu ERP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the results">
    Once complete, the current-stock table will populate with the imported quantities. Check a sample of SKUs against your Shiprocket dashboard to confirm accuracy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Syncing Warehouses from Shiprocket

Hitayu ERP mirrors your Shiprocket warehouse locations so that stock is always attributed to the correct physical site. When you add a new fulfilment centre in Shiprocket, you must sync it into Hitayu ERP before that location appears in stock views and purchase orders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger a warehouse sync">
    From any Inventory page, click **Sync Warehouses** in the toolbar. This calls the Shiprocket API and fetches the latest list of your registered warehouse locations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review new locations">
    Any newly discovered warehouses are added to your warehouse list automatically. Existing warehouses are updated with any name or address changes from Shiprocket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the warehouse list">
    Navigate to **Admin → Warehouses** to review the full list and ensure all locations are correctly labelled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Warehouse sync requires the **Inventory: Manage Warehouses** and **Settings: Integrations** permissions. Ensure your Shiprocket API credentials are configured under **Settings → Integrations** before running a sync.
</Note>
