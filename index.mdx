Hitayu ERP is a full-stack CRM and order management system built for e-commerce businesses that run on Shopify. It connects every stage of your operations — from the moment an order lands in Shopify, through calling and payment collection, packing, Shiprocket dispatch, and final delivery — into a single, role-aware platform your entire team can use.

## Get Up and Running in 4 Steps

Follow these steps to go from a fresh login to actively processing orders with your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to Hitayu ERP">
    Navigate to your Hitayu ERP URL in a browser and sign in with the email address and password provided by your administrator. Accounts are created centrally — there is no self-registration. See [Login & Account Access](/getting-started/login) for full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Your Dashboard">
    After signing in you will land on the dashboard most relevant to your role — either the IVR Dashboard for calling teams or the Shopify Dashboard for e-commerce operations. Familiarise yourself with the metrics and navigation sidebar before diving in. See [Dashboard Overview](/getting-started/dashboard-overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Shopify Store">
    An administrator needs to link your Shopify store via **Settings → Integrations** so that orders, products, and customers sync automatically into Hitayu ERP. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview) for the step-by-step setup guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Managing Orders">
    With your store connected, incoming Shopify orders will appear in the Orders module. Your calling team can work the IVR queue, your packing team can pick and pack confirmed orders, and your shipping team can push shipments to Shiprocket — all from the same platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>
