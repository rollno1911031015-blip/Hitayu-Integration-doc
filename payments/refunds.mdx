Refunds in Hitayu ERP allow you to return captured online payments to customers when an order is cancelled after payment has been collected, or when a return is approved. Currently, refunds are supported for orders paid via **PayU**. The entire refund process - from initiation to confirmation - is managed from the order detail page without needing to log into your gateway dashboard.

## When to Issue a Refund

You should initiate a refund when:

- A prepaid order is cancelled after payment has been successfully captured.
- A customer returns a delivered item and your policy entitles them to a monetary refund (as opposed to store credit or replacement).
- A duplicate payment has been collected by mistake.

<Note>
  Refunds apply only to **Paid** prepaid orders. COD orders and orders with a **Pending** or **Failed** payment status do not require a gateway refund - no funds have been captured in those cases.
</Note>

## How to Initiate a PayU Refund

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Go to **Orders** in the left sidebar and click on the order for which you want to issue a refund. Confirm that the payment status shows **Paid**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Refund Option">
    Scroll to the **Payment** section on the order detail page. Click the **Refund via PayU** button. This is only visible on orders with a captured PayU payment and requires the `payments.refund` permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Refund">
    A confirmation dialog will appear showing the refund amount and the PayU transaction reference. Review the details carefully, then click **Confirm Refund** to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refund Submitted">
    Hitayu ERP sends the refund request to PayU. The payment status on the order updates to **Refunded** once PayU acknowledges the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Refunds are irreversible.** Once confirmed, the refund request is sent immediately to the payment gateway and cannot be cancelled or recalled. Double-check the order and amount before clicking Confirm Refund.
</Warning>

## Refund Status Tracking

After initiating a refund, you can track its progress in two places:

- **Order Detail -> Payment Section** - The payment status badge changes from **Paid** to **Refunded**. The timestamp of the refund initiation is also recorded.
- **Payments Page** - Filter by **Status: Refunded** to see all refunded transactions. Each row shows the gateway reference ID which you can use to cross-check in the PayU dashboard.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paid** | Refund has not yet been initiated. |
| **Refunded** | Refund has been successfully submitted to the gateway. |

<Note>
  There is no intermediate "Refund Processing" status in Hitayu ERP. The status moves directly to **Refunded** once the gateway acknowledges the request. The actual credit to the customer's account happens on the gateway's own timeline.
</Note>

## How Long Do Refunds Take?

Refund timelines are determined by the payment gateway and the customer's bank - Hitayu ERP has no control over how quickly funds appear in the customer's account.

| Gateway | Typical Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| **PayU** | 5-7 business days to the original payment instrument |

<Tip>
  If a customer reports that they haven't received their refund after 7 business days, share the gateway transaction ID (visible on the order detail page) with them so they can raise a query directly with their bank or card issuer.
</Tip>

## Permissions Required

Initiating a refund requires the **`payments.refund`** permission. If you do not see the Refund button on a paid order, contact your workspace administrator to check your role permissions.
