Payment links let your team collect prepaid payments from customers without requiring them to visit your website. From any order detail page, you can generate a secure checkout link and share it over WhatsApp or SMS in seconds — the customer taps the link, completes payment on a hosted checkout page, and the order status updates automatically.

## How Payment Links Work

When you create a payment link for an order, Hitayu ERP generates a unique, single-use checkout URL tied to that order's amount and reference number. The link routes the customer to the appropriate gateway checkout page:

- **PayU orders** — customer lands on `/payu/checkout/{order}`
- **Cashfree orders** — customer lands on `/cashfree/checkout/{order}`
- **Razorpay orders** — customer completes payment through the Razorpay-hosted flow

Once the customer submits payment, the gateway sends a response back to Hitayu ERP, the payment status updates to **Paid**, and the order moves to the fulfillment queue automatically.

## Sending a Payment Link — Pay Now

Use this flow when you want the customer to pay immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Navigate to **Orders** and click on the order you want to collect payment for. The order must be in a state where payment has not yet been completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pay Now">
    In the **Payment** section of the order detail page, click the **Pay Now** button. Hitayu ERP generates a checkout link for the order amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delivery Channel">
    Select whether to send the link via **WhatsApp** or **SMS**. The customer's registered mobile number is pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Link">
    Confirm and send. The customer receives the message within seconds containing the payment link and order summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Completion">
    The payment status on the order detail page updates in real time. Once the customer pays, the status moves from **Pending** to **Paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scheduling a Payment Link — Pay Later

Use the Pay Later flow when you want to generate a link now but send it at a scheduled time — for example, after a customer confirms their order over a call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Go to **Orders** and open the relevant order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Pay Later">
    In the **Payment** section, choose **Pay Later** instead of Pay Now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Schedule">
    Pick the date and time at which the payment link notification should be sent to the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Save the schedule. Hitayu ERP will automatically dispatch the payment link message at the chosen time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Pay Later is especially useful for telesales teams that take orders over the phone during off-hours and want customers to receive the link at a more convenient time.
</Tip>

## Resending a Payment Link

If a customer says they didn't receive the link, or the previous link expired, you can resend it instantly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Navigate to the order detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Resend Payment Link">
    In the **Payment** section, click **Resend Payment Link**. This triggers the `orders.resend-payment-link` action and sends a fresh link to the customer's registered number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Resending a payment link invalidates the previous link. If the customer tries to use the old link after a resend, they will see an error and should use the new one.
</Note>

## The Customer's Experience

When a customer receives a payment link:

1. **WhatsApp** — They receive a templated WhatsApp message containing the order summary and a tap-to-pay button.
2. **SMS** — They receive a text message with the checkout URL.
3. They tap the link, which opens the gateway's hosted checkout page in their browser.
4. They choose their preferred payment method (UPI, card, net banking, wallet) and complete payment.
5. On success, they are redirected to a confirmation page and receive a payment receipt.

No account creation or login is required for the customer.

## Tracking Payment Link Status

You can monitor the status of every payment link from two places:

- **Order Detail → Payment Section** — Shows the current payment status (`pending`, `paid`, `failed`) and the timestamp of the last status change.
- **Payments Page** — Filter by **Status: Pending** to see all orders waiting for payment link completion.

## Partial Payment Links

For high-value orders, you may want to collect a partial advance payment upfront and the balance later.

<Note>
  Partial payment link support depends on your gateway configuration and your plan. Contact your account manager to enable partial payment collection for your workspace.
</Note>

When partial payments are enabled:

- You can specify the amount to collect (e.g., 50% advance).
- A separate payment record is created for each partial payment against the same order.
- The order moves to **Paid** only when the full order value has been collected across all payment records.

<Warning>
  Do not dispatch an order until the full payment amount has been captured. Always verify the **Paid** status on the order before pushing it for shipment.
</Warning>
