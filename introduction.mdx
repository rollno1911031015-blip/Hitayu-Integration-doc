Hitayu ERP is an all-in-one CRM and order management platform designed specifically for e-commerce businesses that sell through Shopify and fulfil orders via Shiprocket. Rather than juggling separate tools for your calling team, payment follow-up, warehouse operations, and courier management, Hitayu ERP brings every department into a single system — with role-based access ensuring each team member sees exactly what they need.

## Who Is Hitayu ERP For?

Hitayu ERP serves the entire staff of a direct-to-consumer e-commerce business. Typical users include:

- **Administrators** who manage users, roles, integrations, and company-wide settings.
- **Sales and calling agents** who work the IVR calling queue, confirm orders, collect payments, and handle lead follow-ups.
- **Packing team members** who receive confirmed orders and prepare them for dispatch.
- **Shipping team members** who push packed orders to Shiprocket and track shipments through to delivery.
- **Finance and reporting staff** who review payment records, reconcile collections, and download operational reports.

## The Order Lifecycle

Every order processed through Hitayu ERP follows a defined lifecycle. Understanding this flow will help you know which module to use at each stage.

| Stage | What Happens | Key Module |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Shopify Intake** | Orders and abandoned checkouts sync automatically from your connected Shopify store into Hitayu ERP. | Orders |
| **2. Calling** | Calling agents work the IVR queue — confirming orders, handling objections, and recording payment intent (pay now, pay later, COD). | IVR Calling |
| **3. Payment** | Payment links are sent to customers via Cashfree or PayU. Agents track paid, partially paid, and pending statuses. | Payments |
| **4. Packing** | Confirmed and paid orders are passed to the packing team who pick items, verify quantities, and mark orders as packed. | Packing |
| **5. Shiprocket Shipping** | Packed orders are pushed to Shiprocket to create shipments, assign courier partners, and generate AWB numbers. | Shipments |
| **6. Delivery** | Shipments are tracked in real time through to successful delivery at the customer's address. | Shipments |
| **7. NDR / RTO** | Non-delivery reports and return-to-origin shipments are flagged for agent follow-up or disposal. | Shipments / Calling |
