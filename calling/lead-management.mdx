Leads in Hitayu represent potential customers who have shown interest but have not yet placed a confirmed order. Unlike orders, which are fully committed transactions, leads are prospective - they need nurturing through follow-ups, status updates, and timely outreach before they convert. Hitayu captures leads automatically from multiple sources and gives your team a single workspace to track, qualify, and convert them.

## Leads vs. Orders

| | Lead | Order |
|-|------|-------|
| **What it is** | A prospect or enquiry | A confirmed purchase transaction |
| **Payment status** | Pending, partial, or committed | Paid, COD, or payment-linked |
| **Primary action** | Qualify, nurture, convert | Fulfil, ship, support |
| **Where to find it** | `/leads` | `/admin/orders/{segment}` |

Once a lead is successfully converted, Hitayu links it to the resulting order and marks the lead status as **Converted to Order**.

---

## Lead Sources

Hitayu captures leads from five sources. The source is recorded on every lead so you always know where a prospect came from.

| Source | How It Works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Shopify** | Abandoned checkouts and new customer sign-ups are pulled in automatically via the Shopify integration |
| **IVR** | Inbound calls that do not match an existing order create a new lead automatically |
| **Manual** | Team members can create a lead directly from the Leads list page |
| **WhatsApp** | Incoming WhatsApp enquiries can be linked to leads |
| **Website** | Contact or enquiry forms on your website can be integrated to push leads into Hitayu |

## Navigating the Leads List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Leads Section">
    Go to **Leads** in the main navigation, or navigate directly to `/leads`. The full leads list loads with your most recent leads at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by Segment">
    Use the segment navigation to filter by lead status or source. Each segment has its own URL at `/leads/segment/{segment}`, making it easy to bookmark specific views such as "New Leads" or "Follow Up".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and Sort">
    Use the search bar to find leads by customer name, phone number, or email. Sort columns by date created, status, or assignee to prioritise your work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing a Lead Detail Page

Click on any lead in the list to open its full detail page at `/leads/{lead}`. The detail page is your central workspace for everything related to that prospect.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lead Information" icon="address-card">
    Customer contact details, the originating source, associated product or product variant, and the date the lead was created.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Status & Assignment" icon="tag">
    Current lead status, the assigned team member, assignment history, and the date the lead was last updated.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Activities Log" icon="list-check">
    A chronological log of every interaction - status changes, notes added, assignments made, and calls linked to this lead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follow-ups" icon="calendar-check">
    Scheduled callbacks and their completion status. See [Followups](/calling/followups) for full details.

## Updating Lead Status

Keeping statuses accurate is the foundation of a healthy pipeline. Hitayu ships with a set of built-in statuses, and admins can add custom ones to match your team's workflow.

### Built-in Lead Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **New** | Lead just created; not yet reviewed |
| **Assigned** | A team member has been assigned to work this lead |
| **Follow Up** | A callback or follow-up action is scheduled |
| **Payment Pending** | Customer is interested but payment has not been received |
| **Partial Paid** | Customer has paid a deposit or partial amount |
| **Converted to Order** | Lead has been successfully converted into an order |
| **Rejected** | Lead was disqualified (wrong number, not interested, etc.) |
| **Cancelled** | Lead was closed without conversion |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Lead">
    From `/leads`, click the lead you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a New Status">
    On the lead detail page, click the **Status** dropdown and select the appropriate status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Note (Optional but Recommended)">
    Add a note explaining why the status changed. This note is saved to the **Activities Log** so the full team has context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Action">
    Click **Save** or **Submit Action**. The status update and note are recorded instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding Notes to a Lead

Notes let you capture anything relevant - a customer's concerns, a promised callback time, or a product preference - without changing the lead status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Lead Detail Page">
    Navigate to `/leads/{lead}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Notes / Action Panel">
    Scroll to the action panel at the bottom of the page. Select **Add Note** from the action type options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and Submit">
    Type your note and click **Submit**. The note is appended to the Activities Log with your name and a timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Write notes in full sentences with enough context for a colleague to understand the situation without needing to call the customer again. Good notes save time and reduce duplicate outreach.
</Tip>

## Assigning Leads to Team Members

Leads can be assigned manually to any active team member. Assignments are tracked so you can see the full history of who owned a lead at each stage.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Lead">
    Go to `/leads/{lead}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Assign from the Action Panel">
    In the action panel, choose **Assign** as the action type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Team Member">
    Select a team member from the dropdown. Only active users with the appropriate permissions appear in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Submit**. The assignee field on the lead updates immediately, and the assignment is logged in the Activities section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Assigning a lead automatically updates its status to **Assigned** if the lead was previously in the **New** state.
</Note>

## Customising Lead Statuses (Admin)

If your business uses terminology or pipeline stages that differ from the built-in statuses, admins can create custom lead statuses that appear alongside the defaults.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Lead Status Masters">
    Go to **Masters -> Lead Statuses** or navigate to `/masters/lead-statuses`. This page requires admin or masters-level permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a New Status">
    Click **Add Status**. Enter a status name and (optionally) a colour code so it is visually distinct in the leads list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Use">
    Click **Save**. The new status becomes available immediately in the lead status dropdown for all team members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting or renaming an existing status that is already assigned to leads will not retroactively change those leads - they will retain the old status label until manually updated.
</Warning>
