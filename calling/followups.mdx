A follow-up in Hitayu is a scheduled callback that an agent creates when a customer is not ready to confirm or pay during an initial call. Rather than losing track of the interaction, the agent logs a promise — a specific date and time to call the customer back — and Hitayu surfaces that reminder automatically when the time comes. Following up consistently and on time is one of the most reliable ways to improve order conversion rates.

## What Followups Are and When to Use Them

Follow-ups are created as the outcome of a call action. Use a follow-up when:

- The customer asks you to call them back at a specific time ("Call me tomorrow morning").
- You could not reach the customer and want to try again at a set time.
- The customer needs time to check with someone else before confirming.
- A payment commitment was made but has not yet been fulfilled and needs a gentle reminder.

<Note>
  A follow-up is not a permanent deferral — it is a time-bound commitment with a clear next action. Every follow-up should have a scheduled date/time and a brief note explaining the reason.
</Note>

## Scheduling a Followup from a Call Action

Follow-ups are created directly from the calling queue, immediately after a call ends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the Call">
    Finish your conversation with the customer and return to the call card in your calling queue at `/admin/calling`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Follow-Up Action">
    In the call action panel, choose **Follow Up** from the list of available actions (Pay Now, Pay Later, Follow Up, Cancel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Callback Date and Time">
    Use the date and time picker to select exactly when you — or a colleague — should call the customer back. Be as specific as possible; a vague date is harder to prioritise than an exact time slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Note">
    In the notes field, briefly describe what the customer said and what the purpose of the callback is. For example: "Customer wants to confirm quantity with spouse. Call back Wednesday 10 AM."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Follow-Up">
    Click **Save**. The follow-up is now logged against the lead or order and will appear in the reminder queue when the scheduled time arrives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If the customer gave you a specific time window, set the follow-up to the start of that window rather than the end. Calling a few minutes early is always better than missing the window.
</Tip>

## The Follow-Up Reminder Queue

When a scheduled follow-up is due, it surfaces automatically in the dedicated reminder queue. Agents can access this view to see all callbacks that are ready to action right now.

**URL:** `/ecommerce.follow-up-reminder`

This page pre-filters the calling queue to show only calls and leads that have an active, due follow-up action — so there is no manual searching required. The reminder queue works the same way as the main calling queue; each item shows the customer name, contact details, the original call notes, and the follow-up reason.

| Queue View | URL | What It Shows |
|------------|-----|---------------|
| Full Calling Queue | `/ecommerce.calling-queue` | All calls assigned to you |
| Follow-Up Reminders | `/ecommerce.follow-up-reminder` | Only due follow-up callbacks |
| Pay Now | `/ecommerce.create-order-pay-now` | Calls awaiting payment confirmation |
| Pay Later | `/ecommerce.create-order-pay-later` | Calls with a pay-later commitment |
| Cancelled | `/ecommerce.cancel-with-remarks` | Calls marked as cancelled |

## Follow-Up Statuses

Every follow-up has one of three statuses that reflects where it stands in its lifecycle.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | The follow-up is scheduled but the callback has not happened yet |
| **Completed** | The agent called the customer and the follow-up was resolved |
| **Missed** | The scheduled time passed without the callback being completed |

<Warning>
  Missed follow-ups represent broken promises to customers. Monitor the missed follow-up count on the manager dashboard regularly and work to keep it as close to zero as possible.
</Warning>

## Completing or Rescheduling a Followup

When you reach a customer on a follow-up call, you close the loop by marking the follow-up as completed and recording a new call action.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reminder Queue">
    Go to `/ecommerce.follow-up-reminder` to see all due callbacks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call the Customer">
    Use the phone number displayed on the follow-up card to place the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the Outcome">
    After the call, select an action:
    - **Pay Now** — Customer is ready to pay. Send the payment link.
    - **Pay Later** — Customer confirms payment for a future date.
    - **Follow Up** — Customer still needs more time. Schedule another callback.
    - **Cancel** — Customer declines. Record a reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as Completed">
    Once you record an outcome action, Hitayu automatically marks the follow-up as **Completed** and logs the completion timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**If the customer is still not ready**, use the **Follow Up** action again to schedule another reminder. Each rescheduled follow-up creates a new entry in the follow-up timeline, so the full history of attempts is always visible on the lead detail page.

<Note>
  You can reschedule a follow-up as many times as needed, but be mindful of how many attempts are reasonable for a single lead. After several missed contacts, it may be more appropriate to mark the lead as **Rejected** and refocus your time on warmer prospects.
</Note>

## Manager View: All Team Followups

Managers have visibility into the follow-up activity of the entire team, not just their own queue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the IVR Dashboard">
    Navigate to `/ivr-dashboard`. This page requires the `dashboard.ivr` permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by Follow-Up Status">
    Use the status filters to view **Pending**, **Completed**, or **Missed** follow-ups across all agents. You can also filter by date range or individual agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reassign Overdue Follow-Ups">
    If an agent is unavailable and has pending follow-ups that are overdue, use the assign action on each call to transfer it to an available agent immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Run a quick end-of-day review of **Missed** follow-ups so that any broken callbacks can be rescheduled for the next morning before they grow cold.
</Tip>

## Best Practices for Timely Follow-Ups

Consistent, well-timed follow-ups directly improve your team's conversion rate. Here are the habits that make the biggest difference.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Follow Up Within the Hour" icon="clock">
    Research consistently shows that leads contacted within an hour of their initial enquiry are far more likely to convert. Set your follow-up reminders to land as soon as the customer is available.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Always Log a Note" icon="pencil">
    Write a short note on every follow-up explaining why the callback is needed. This helps any teammate who picks up the call to continue the conversation naturally without making the customer repeat themselves.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep the Queue Clean" icon="broom">
    Resolve or reschedule every pending follow-up before the end of the day. An overflowing reminder queue loses urgency; a clean queue makes every item feel important.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Three Attempts, Then Decide" icon="phone-slash">
    If you have made three attempts with no response, make a deliberate decision: schedule one final attempt at a very different time of day, or mark the lead as rejected and move on. Avoid indefinite callbacks that clog the queue.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
