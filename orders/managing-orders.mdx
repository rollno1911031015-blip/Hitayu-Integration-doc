Day-to-day order management in Hitayu ERP is built around a single order detail page that brings together everything your team needs: the customer's information, ordered items, payment links, shipping details, activity log, and every available action — all without leaving the screen. This guide walks through each operation your team performs regularly, in the order you are most likely to encounter them during a shift.

---

## Viewing an Order Detail Page

To open an order, click its row in any order list. The detail page is organised into sections:

- **Order Summary** — order number, source (Shopify or manual), date, assigned team member, and current status badge
- **Customer & Shipping** — customer name, phone, email, and the delivery address on file
- **Line Items** — each product, variant, quantity, unit price, discounts, taxes, and the order grand total
- **Payment Links** — all payment links ever generated for this order, with their amounts, statuses, and direct URLs
- **Shipment Details** — AWB number, courier name, Shiprocket order ID, and a tracking link once the shipment is live
- **Activity Log** — a chronological feed of every action taken on the order
- **Status History** — every status transition with timestamps and responsible team members
- **Edit History** — a record of every change made to items, address, or totals

## Assigning an Order to a Team Member

New orders imported from Shopify are automatically assigned to an available order-staff agent using round-robin rotation. You can change the assignment at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Navigate to the order detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign">
    Find the **Assign** action in the order actions panel. A dropdown will appear showing all eligible team members within your data scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Assignee">
    Choose the team member you want to assign the order to. Optionally add a remark explaining the reason for the assignment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Assign**. The order's assignee field updates immediately and the change is recorded in the activity log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Round-robin auto-assignment runs when an order first arrives from Shopify. It distributes orders evenly across all active agents with the `order_staff` role. Manual reassignment overrides this and is tracked separately.
</Note>

## Converting an Abandoned Order to Confirmed

Abandoned checkouts and unconfirmed orders can be converted into active confirmed orders once the customer has indicated they want to proceed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Abandoned Order">
    Go to **Abandoned Only** or **Abandoned Orders** in the segment tabs to locate the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Order Detail">
    Click the order row to open its detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Convert">
    Press the **Convert** button. Enter a short remark (e.g. "Customer confirmed via call — wants to pay online") and submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Payment Flow">
    Immediately after converting you will typically choose how the customer will pay — see the **Sending a Payment Link** section below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sending and Resending Payment Links

Hitayu ERP offers three payment flows to match different customer situations. All three send the customer a link they can use to complete payment on their own device.

### Pay Now — Full Payment

Use this when the customer is ready to pay the full order amount immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order and Click Pay Now">
    On the order detail page, select **Pay Now** from the actions panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Remark (Optional)">
    Enter a note if needed, then confirm. Hitayu ERP generates a PayU payment link for the full order amount and sends it to the customer's registered phone or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer Completes Payment">
    Once the customer pays, the order's payment status automatically updates to **Paid** and the order advances to **Verification Pending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pay Later — COD / Deferred Payment

Use this when the customer agrees to pay on delivery or wants to defer payment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order and Click Pay Later">
    Select **Pay Later** from the actions panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Add a remark and confirm. The order is marked as COD, payment status is set to **Pending**, and a shipment placeholder is created automatically so the warehouse can begin fulfilment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pay Partial — Split Payment

Use this when the customer wants to pay a deposit now and the remainder on delivery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order and Click Pay Partial">
    Select **Pay Partial** from the actions panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Partial Amount">
    Type in the deposit amount. The system enforces commercial rules — minimums and maximums depend on the order total and your configured thresholds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Submit the form. A payment link is generated for the partial amount and sent to the customer. The order records both the amount collected and the outstanding balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Resending a Payment Link

If a customer says they did not receive a link, or the link has expired:

1. Open the order detail page.
2. In the **Payment Links** section, find the active link and click **Resend**.
3. Hitayu ERP re-delivers the existing link to the customer — no new link is created unless the previous one is no longer active.

<Warning>
You can only resend a link that is in **Created** or **Sent** status. Links in **Paid** or **Failed** status cannot be resent. If a link has failed, use **Pay Now** to generate a fresh one.
</Warning>

## Editing Order Details

You can modify an order's items, quantities, address, or totals at any time before the shipment is picked up. Changes are synced back to Shopify if the Shopify order-edit sync integration is enabled for your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order and Click Edit">
    From the order detail page, click the **Edit Order** button. You need the `orders.edit` permission to see this button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Modify Items or Address">
    Add or remove line items, change quantities, update the shipping address, or adjust discounts. The order total recalculates in real time as you make changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Payment Action">
    Before saving you must decide what to do about payment:
    - **Save Only** — save the changes without touching the payment link
    - **Pay Now** — save and immediately generate a new full-payment link
    - **Partial Payment** — save and generate a partial-payment link for the revised amount
    - **Pay Later** — save and mark the revised order as COD
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Update Order**. The system saves the edit, records a snapshot in Edit History, and (if applicable) sends the new payment link to the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Every edit is captured in the **Edit History** tab with a full before-and-after snapshot: previous totals, new totals, previous discount, new discount, shipping charges, and tax — plus the name of the team member who made the change.
</Note>

## Pushing an Order to Shiprocket

Once an order is verified and packed, push it to Shiprocket to create the shipment and request courier pickup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the Order is Verified">
    The order must be in **Verified** status (or further along) before Shiprocket push is available. If it is still pending verification, complete that step first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Push to Shiprocket">
    On the order detail page, click **Push to Shiprocket**. Add an optional remark and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Result">
    If Shiprocket is configured and credentials are valid, the shipment is created instantly and you will see the Shiprocket Order ID appear in the shipment section. The order status advances to **Shipment Created**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If you see the message "Shipment placeholder created — enable Shiprocket settings to push it live", it means your Shiprocket API credentials or order-creation settings have not been configured. Contact your administrator to complete the Shiprocket integration setup before retrying.
</Warning>

## Rejecting or Cancelling an Order

### Rejecting an Order

Rejection is used when a customer actively declines the order during a follow-up call, or when the order cannot proceed for a legitimate reason.

1. Open the order detail page.
2. Click **Reject Order** in the actions panel. You need the `orders.delete` permission.
3. Enter the customer's reason or a staff remark in the remarks field.
4. Confirm. The order moves to **Cancelled** status with the rejection reason saved against it.

### Cancelling an Order

Cancellation follows the same flow as rejection but is typically initiated by staff rather than the customer. The order will appear in the **Cancelled with Remarks** segment if a remark was recorded.

<Tip>
Always enter a clear remark when rejecting or cancelling. The remark is visible in the activity log and helps managers understand rejection patterns when reviewing team performance or product feedback.
</Tip>

## Processing a Refund

For orders where payment was collected through PayU and the order is subsequently cancelled or returned:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order and Click Refund">
    Select **Refund (PayU)** from the actions panel. You need the `payments.refund` permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Refund Amount">
    Type the amount to refund. This can be the full order amount or a partial amount for partial refunds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Confirmation and Remarks">
    Provide the confirmation text required by the refund form and add a reason for the refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Refund**. Hitayu ERP queues the refund request with PayU and records the refund details (including the PayU request reference) in the activity log. The order's payment status updates to **Refunded** once PayU processes the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Calling the Customer

If your account has IVR calling configured, you can queue a call to the customer directly from the order detail page.

1. Click **Call Customer** on the order detail page.
2. The system queues an IVR call and assigns it to the next available calling agent using the same round-robin logic as order assignment.
3. A confirmation message shows you which agent the call was assigned to.

## Order Edit History (Audit Trail)

The **Edit History** tab on every order detail page shows a complete log of every manual change made to the order after it was created. Each entry records:

- **Date and time** of the edit
- **Team member** who made the change
- **Previous totals** vs **new totals** (subtotal, shipping, discount, tax, grand total)
- A full **before snapshot** and **after snapshot** of the order's items and address at the time of the edit

This provides an immutable audit trail for disputes, returns, or financial reconciliation.

---

## Order Activity Log

Below the order detail, the **Activity Log** tab shows a time-ordered feed of every event that has ever occurred on the order, including:

- Order creation and Shopify import events
- Status transitions (with the responsible team member)
- Payment link generation and delivery confirmations
- Assignment changes
- Refund submissions
- Manual notes added by staff

<Tip>
Use the activity log as your first stop when investigating a customer complaint. It gives you the full story of an order in chronological order without needing to dig through multiple reports.
</Tip>
