An abandoned cart represents a customer who showed genuine purchase intent - they browsed, chose products, and started checkout - but did not complete the transaction. Hitayu ERP automatically imports these abandoned Shopify checkouts alongside regular orders, giving your calling and recovery team a structured queue to work from without any manual data entry. This page explains what abandoned carts are in the context of Hitayu ERP, how the system handles them, and how your team can convert them into confirmed, paid orders.

---

## What Are Abandoned Carts in Hitayu ERP?

When a customer on your Shopify storefront adds items to their cart and starts the checkout process but leaves without paying, Shopify captures the partial checkout as an abandoned checkout record. Hitayu ERP's Shopify integration imports these records automatically - just like it imports paid orders - and treats them as a distinct order type requiring recovery action.

An abandoned order in Hitayu ERP has:
- The customer's name, phone number, and email (if provided at checkout)
- The items they intended to buy, with quantities and prices
- A shipping address if the customer progressed far enough to enter one
- A direct link back to the Shopify checkout so the customer can complete it independently

<Note>
Not all abandoned records come from partial checkouts. Some represent full Shopify orders that were later flagged as at-risk or unconfirmed. Both types appear in the abandoned segments and are handled the same way.
</Note>

## How Hitayu ERP Imports Abandoned Checkouts

Hitayu ERP uses a real-time Shopify webhook listener to receive abandoned checkout notifications. The import process runs automatically:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shopify Sends the Webhook">
    Shopify fires an `orders/create` or abandoned checkout webhook to Hitayu ERP's endpoint as soon as a checkout is flagged as abandoned (typically within a few hours of inactivity).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer and Address Are Resolved">
    Hitayu ERP matches the incoming payload to an existing customer record by Shopify customer ID and channel. If the customer does not exist yet, a new record is created automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order Record Is Created">
    The order is saved with status `SHOPIFY_RECEIVED` and its source is tagged to indicate it came from an abandoned checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic Assignment">
    The system immediately applies round-robin assignment to put the order in an agent's queue so no abandoned checkout sits unattended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lead Is Created (If Unpaid)">
    For unpaid abandoned checkouts, Hitayu ERP also creates or updates a lead record in the CRM so the recovery team has it in their follow-up pipeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing Abandoned Orders

The **Orders** section has two dedicated segments for abandoned cart recovery:

| Segment | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Abandoned Orders** | All orders originating from abandoned Shopify checkouts - including those that have already been converted to confirmed orders. Use this for a full picture of your abandoned cart volume. |
| **Abandoned Only** | Strictly unconverted abandoned checkouts that are still awaiting recovery action. Use this as your active recovery work queue. |

<Tip>
Work from **Abandoned Only** during your recovery shift - it shows only the orders that still need action. Switch to **Abandoned Orders** when you want to review overall abandoned cart performance or check on previously converted orders.
</Tip>

The **Recovery Orders** summary card at the top of the orders list also gives you a live count of how many abandoned and at-risk orders are currently open, helping managers assess recovery team capacity at a glance.

## Recovering an Abandoned Cart: Step-by-Step

There are two main paths for recovering an abandoned checkout: sending an online payment link (pay-now or pay-partial) or confirming the order as cash-on-delivery (pay-later). Both paths start the same way.

### Step 1 - Find and Open the Order

Navigate to **Abandoned Only** and click the order you want to work on. Review the customer details, the items in the cart, and the order total before making contact.

### Step 2 - Contact the Customer

Use the **Call Customer** button to queue an IVR call, or contact the customer through your preferred channel. During the conversation, confirm:
- That they still want the items
- Their preferred payment method
- Whether the shipping address is correct

### Step 3 - Choose a Recovery Path

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pay Now (Online Payment)" icon="credit-card">
    The customer wants to pay online immediately. Click **Pay Now**, add a remark, and confirm. Hitayu ERP converts the order to **Confirmed**, generates an online payment link for the full amount, and sends it to the customer's phone or email. The customer completes payment on their own device.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pay Later (COD)" icon="truck">
    The customer prefers cash-on-delivery. Click **Pay Later**, add a remark, and confirm. The order is marked as **Confirmed** with COD payment mode and a shipment placeholder is created so the warehouse can begin fulfilment without waiting for online payment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pay Partial (Deposit)" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    The customer wants to pay a deposit now and the balance on delivery. Click **Pay Partial**, enter the deposit amount, and confirm. A partial payment link is sent to the customer. The remaining balance is tracked against the order.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reject Order" icon="xmark">
    The customer explicitly does not want to proceed. Click **Reject Order**, enter the reason, and confirm. The order moves to **Cancelled** and appears in the **Customer Rejected** segment for reporting.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The Calling Team's Role in Recovery

Your calling team is the primary driver of abandoned cart recovery. Hitayu ERP is designed to minimise the manual work required so agents can focus on the conversation rather than data entry.

**What the calling team does:**
1. Works through the **Abandoned Only** queue in order of assignment
2. Calls the customer to understand why they did not complete checkout
3. Addresses objections (price concerns, product questions, delivery queries)
4. Selects the appropriate payment path in Hitayu ERP while the customer is on the line
5. Confirms the customer has received and opened the payment link before ending the call
6. Records a clear remark if the order cannot be recovered (objection type, customer preference)

**What Hitayu ERP does automatically:**
- Assigns new abandoned checkouts to agents in round-robin order
- Creates a CRM lead for each unpaid abandoned checkout so nothing falls through the cracks
- Tracks the payment link status so the agent can tell in real time whether the customer has paid
- Records every call attempt and action in the activity log for manager review

<Note>
Agents must have the `orders.verify` permission to perform Convert, Pay Now, Pay Later, Pay Partial, and Resend Payment Link actions on abandoned orders.
</Note>

## Resending a Payment Link

If the customer says they did not receive the link, or asks you to send it again:

1. Open the order detail page.
2. In the **Payment Links** section, locate the active link (status: **Created** or **Sent**).
3. Click **Resend**. The system re-delivers the link to the customer's contact details.

If no active link exists, use **Pay Now** to generate a fresh one.

## Best Practices for Abandoned Cart Follow-Up

Following up at the right time and in the right way significantly improves conversion rates. Here are guidelines based on common e-commerce recovery patterns.

### Timing

| Time Since Abandonment | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| **0-1 hour** | Send an automated reminder (if configured via Shopify flows or marketing tools) |
| **1-4 hours** | First call attempt - this is the highest-conversion window; the purchase is still top of mind |
| **4-24 hours** | Second call attempt if the first was unanswered |
| **24-48 hours** | Final follow-up call; after this point conversion rates drop significantly |
| **48+ hours** | Mark as unrecoverable unless the customer reaches out independently |

### During the Call

- **Lead with the product, not the cart.** Remind the customer what they were interested in rather than opening with "you didn't complete your purchase."
- **Offer options.** If price is the objection, mention the partial payment option. If urgency is low, highlight expected delivery timelines.
- **Send the link while on the call.** Click **Pay Now** (or the appropriate option) while the customer is still engaged and confirm they can see the link before hanging up.
- **Record every outcome.** Whether the order converts or is rejected, add a remark. These notes feed into reporting that helps your team refine the recovery script over time.

### Segment Management

<Tip>
Keep the **Abandoned Only** segment empty at the end of each shift. Every order in that queue should either be in the process of being recovered (a payment link has been sent) or have been explicitly rejected with a reason. An unworked abandoned order is lost revenue.
</Tip>

### Avoiding Over-Contact

<Warning>
Do not call the same customer more than twice in a 24-hour period for a single abandoned order. Excessive contact harms customer trust and brand perception. If two attempts go unanswered, log a remark and let the automated marketing sequences (if configured) handle further follow-up.
</Warning>
